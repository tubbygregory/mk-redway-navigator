## Prioritised findings

1. **High — precise address results can lose their number or be discarded.** A returned name equal to the road suppresses the `house_number + road` label. Deduplication sorts by distance and merges same-name results within 700 metres, allowing a nearer street to displace an exact address or a nearby branch. Preserve house/address context, source order and distinct destinations; collapse only matching source identity or genuinely equivalent nearby entries. Regression checks cover numbered addresses, streets, nearby branches and local relevance.
2. **Medium — local suggestions are not strictly limited to the product's MK area.** Whole routing ways cross the generator's buffer; 165 of 4,509 existing name midpoints lie outside the supported bounds, yet claim Milton Keynes. Returned rows also lack defensive client-side coordinate validation. Filter local and online results and validate again on selection. Do not apply this restriction to GPX import: outside-MK tracks remain a supported unverified import.
3. **Medium — local search omits places and house addresses already present in the source.** The routing graph deliberately retains highway tags and contains no POI/address index. A read-only scan of the existing Geofabrik input finds approximately 8,000 named places/complete addresses within the current bounds, including Warbler and Bannatyne Health Club. Public Nominatim returns no match for the recorded plural `Bannatynes`, while canonical `Bannatyne` works. Add a small, source-derived index and conservative local punctuation/possessive/plural matching. Keep typing entirely local, preserve online submitted search and avoid additional public fallback requests.
4. **Medium — old result pins survive a new planner search.** Replacing the rows leaves old pins selectable. Clear pins when the rows reset, and skip abandoned geocoder requests before they consume the shared pacing slot. Retain existing revision checks after responses.
5. **Source limitation — number 25 is not explicitly mapped.** Public search returns no Huntley Crescent match. The OSM extract contains three residential buildings with `addr:street=Huntley Crescent`, named `1 - 7`, `8-55` and `56-62`, but no explicit number 25 or verified entrance. Offer source-derived building matches with an explicit approximation warning. Never label a derived building centre as the requested front door.

## Data, scope and privacy

`data/places.json` is a generated browser asset with source identities, names/address tags, MK-bounded coordinates, location precision and provenance. Its validation rejects malformed records, duplicate identities, out-of-bounds points and invalid timestamps. The source PBF replication timestamp is 6 October 2026 at 20:21:06 UTC; HTTP last-modified is a packaging date, not the mapping date. The compact asset is small relative to the existing graph and basemap. No raw PBF, source extract, test, diagnostic or script enters Pages.

The supported bounds remain south 51.955, west -0.905, north 52.155, east -0.615, matching the routing/basemap product. This does not imply coverage of the entire council administrative area or all MK postcodes. OSM attribution and ODbL remain explicit. No council permission or endorsement is claimed.

Local typing and offline search contact no geocoder. Submitted online searches remain bounded to the same area and share forward/reverse request pacing. The app introduces no backend, user account, new dependency, persistent search log or paid external service. Local matches remain useful during empty responses or provider outages, with status wording explaining which source is available.

## Deliberate limits and validation

A national search, paid postal-address provider, address interpolation, invented entrances and geographic boundary expansion are deferred. Complete postal-address coverage would require a separately assessed source, licence, cost and privacy design. Missing source addresses are not an application coordinate bug.

Retain all existing routing/navigation, Cultural Route, GPX, branding and PWA gates. Add focused producer/consumer/request regressions, actual rendered recorded-query tests, malformed/out-of-bounds provider tests and root/subpath offline search. Validate the actual Pages archive against the staged runtime bytes. After squash merge, require main build, deployment, live review and immutable beta release publication; verify production version, place-data hash and source metadata.

Future CI can reuse unchanged geographic outputs when validated source fingerprints match, while retaining freshness checks and all release gates. That optimisation is recorded rather than bypassing the current full build. Chromium iPhone emulation cannot establish physical Safari, installed-iPhone update behaviour, real GPS or cycling accuracy. A physical-device retest of the recorded searches and real entrance selection remains necessary.
