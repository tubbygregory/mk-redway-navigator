Baseline: GitHub main `0bb129e2c26a1c0ddaeaaa05d4850dffab3c98db`, deployed beta v0.14.8. No open PRs; the recent build, deployment, live review and release were successful. The user's 15.7-second installed-iPhone recording demonstrates a successful favourite save followed by repeated unchanged **Save** buttons. Private keyboard autofill content in the recording is not reproduced in this report.

## Review coverage and product boundaries

Reviewed actual rendered search, suggestions/results and pins, selected destinations, planner/alternatives/preferences, route details and More actions, navigation, Saved and favourite shortcuts, Settings/offline/update/install controls, Explore, GPX import/export, sharing, About/Data and map controls. Baseline and repaired screens cover 320/375/390px portrait, 844px landscape and 1280px desktop, light/dark/system/high-contrast appearance and installed-iOS Chromium emulation. The review includes screenshots, native scroll and control hit targets as well as DOM state.

Keep Explore secondary to point-to-point navigation. Preserve the supplied MK logo, primary red, ordinary selected-route colour, installed-iPhone dark surfaces, existing MK bounds, routing v6 schema/weights, source attribution and independent-project disclaimer. GPX files and route names/distances remain source-derived; shortcut files remain open segments. A calculated joining leg must not invent a GPX closing edge, entrance, legal access or official route property. Generic/outside-MK GPX imports retain their existing behaviour.

## Optional opportunities and deliberate limits

- A destination/Saved sheet can cover the section navigation, but normal collapse/close controls remain available. Changing all panel layering requires a separate layout decision; do not present this as a blocked control without evidence.
- Expanded route details put Start/More below the fold on short screens. Native scrolling and the existing collapse handle are the intended access paths; verify them rather than reducing useful route information.
- Custom favourite naming, synced accounts, heritage narration and a broader redesign are outside this focused repair. No backend, new dependency or external commitment is introduced.
- Complete postal-address coverage, physical entrance correctness and all council-area coverage remain source/product limitations. Existing honest approximate-address warnings and local-only typing are retained.
- CI can reuse geographic outputs when source fingerprints and freshness are validated. This review retains the existing full release gates; it does not bypass them for speed.

## Validation and release evidence

Local verification passed Python/data tests (34), JavaScript syntax, representative MK journeys and the complete Node suite (134). Focused rendered checks passed at all five viewport sizes and all appearance choices, including real keyboard focus, immediate More-menu hit targets, red Saved feedback, blocked-storage rollback, Home/Work replacement and native long-held touch release. Cultural regressions cover mode-specific legal joining legs, poor GPS, closed-loop handoff without false arrival, accurate recovery after missed initial fixes, cancellation and unchanged export geometry. All ten source GPX tracks retain their designated colours and unverified-condition labels.

Root/subpath PWA checks passed fresh installation and offline Cultural joining from an explicit start. A real v0.14.8-to-v0.14.9 service-worker upgrade retained Home/favourites and cached graph bytes despite HTTP 503 responses; local address search remained available offline. The staged Pages archive passed the runtime allowlist and byte checks for all 45 files, including routing v6, 8,099 local places, the PMTiles basemap, all ten GPX files and the unchanged approved logo. Geographic data and routing weights were not rebuilt or changed for this repair.
