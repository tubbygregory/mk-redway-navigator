- Current production graph and basemap were downloaded with TLS verification and matched to their published SHA-256 metadata for local checks. Geographic data was not rebuilt locally; Actions retains the normal source refresh and validation.
- The local Pages tar passed the actual archive validator: 33 allowed runtime files, with no source/build inputs. Uploaded archive inspection remains a required GitHub gate.
- Independent cloud-browser HTTPS review hit the environment's certificate trust limitation. Production HTTPS reads with normal TLS verification work; the Actions live-review browser gates remain required before release publication.

## Deliberately deferred and physical checks

- No routing weights, schema version, cultural-route descriptions/distances/links, roundabout exit numbers or geographic properties were invented or changed. Existing GPX validation/export, backend-free sharing, source-failure handling, privacy and attribution behaviour were retained.
- A fuller access hierarchy deserves a separate review: 55 current ways combine general restrictions with specific foot/bicycle permissions. Established routing profiles prioritize specific permissions, but vehicle and directional restrictions also need consideration, and the compact producer does not currently retain their complete hierarchy. This patch retains conservative access filtering rather than making a partial policy change.
- Future CI improvements: cache pinned browser downloads; investigate avoiding forced geographic rebuilds for appearance-only patches; replace the historic branch allowlist with a deliberate PR-check policy. None of these gates were bypassed in this release.
- Physical cycling and an actual installed iPhone remain necessary for GPS jitter, real path/entrance conditions, speech, battery, safe areas, launcher-cache refresh and background/locked-screen recovery.
