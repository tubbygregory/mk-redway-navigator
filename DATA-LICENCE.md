# Map data licensing

MK Redway Navigator uses OpenStreetMap-derived data.

- **Routing network:** derived from OpenStreetMap data and generated by `scripts/build_network.py`.
- **Offline basemap:** a Milton Keynes extract of the Protomaps Basemap, itself derived from OpenStreetMap and distributed as an ODbL Produced Work.
- **Attribution:** the application displays `© OpenStreetMap contributors` on the map and this attribution must be retained.

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Protomaps publishes its basemap as an OpenStreetMap-attributed ODbL Produced Work. The PMTiles software and format are separately open source/public-domain as documented by Protomaps.
