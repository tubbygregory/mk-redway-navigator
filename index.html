Your privacy

Home, Work, favourites and settings stay in this browser. Routes are calculated on your device using the downloaded network. Live location is used for navigation.

Online place and address searches are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service. Map and data downloads contact their hosting providers; if bundled data is unavailable, the app can request map data from external services. There are no user accounts or application backend.

Map and route data

Official route classification: Milton Keynes City Council / Get Around MK, used with permission. Routable topology: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Basemap: Protomaps and its data contributors.
