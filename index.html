Your privacy

Home, Work, favourites and settings stay in this browser. Routes are calculated on your device using the downloaded network. Live location is used for navigation.

Type-ahead suggestions use saved places and bundled MK streets, places and addresses on this device. Typing sends no search requests. Submitted online place and address searches are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service, limited to the app’s MK coverage area; local matches are also available offline. Map and data downloads contact their hosting providers; if bundled data is unavailable, the app can request map data from external services. There are no user accounts or application backend.

Map and route data

Official route classification: Milton Keynes City Council / Get Around MK. Routing topology, mapped places and addresses: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Basemap: Protomaps and its data contributors.

Search covers the MK map area, not every MK postcode or the whole council area. OpenStreetMap addresses can be incomplete. Building and street matches are approximate locations, not verified front doors; choose the actual entrance on the map.

MK Redway Navigator is an independent project and is not an official Milton Keynes City Council service.
