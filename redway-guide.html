Planning a Redway journey

On the Milton Keynes Redway network, the entrance you pick can change the route as much as the destination. Start with the places you can actually enter and leave, then compare the paths between them.

Set your pins where you can enter

A search result for a park, lake or retail site may land in the middle of the mapped area. Zoom in and look for the path entrance on the side you intend to use. Use Choose on map if the result sits behind a fence, beside a grid road or on the wrong side of the water.

For a journey to Willen Lake, the edge of the lake you're heading to is more useful than a pin in the water. If you're meeting someone, agree on the same entrance before sending a route link. For a trip around the lake, use the Yellow Cultural Route for a longer loop through the area or pick a specific path junction as the destination for a shorter outing.

Compare the crossings as well as the time

For cycling, Maximum Redway favours classified Redways, Balanced trades some of that preference for a shorter journey, and Fastest favours a lower estimated travel time. Two options can use the same route. Expand the details to compare road distance, crossings and underpasses before deciding.

If the path and a grid road cross on the map, check whether you need an underpass or a surface crossing. Zoom in on the approach and check signs on arrival. For an evening journey, Prefer lit paths uses mapped lighting where there are records. An unknown lighting entry tells you that the map lacks a lighting record for that stretch.

Allow time for stops and the last stretch

Under Settings → Journey, enter your usual cycling or walking speed in km/h. On a 6 km route, 12 km/h gives about 30 minutes of moving time and 18 km/h gives about 20 minutes. Add time for crossings, meeting someone or stopping to look at landmarks.

A dashed approach is a gap between your pin and the mapped route. The app estimates that stretch at walking pace. If you cannot see a suitable way through, move the pin to an entrance you can verify. A large gap can prevent the app from making a route.

Before leaving home

  1. Select your travel mode and confirm the entrances at both ends.
  2. Check the route details for the road sections or crossings you want to avoid.
  3. Download Offline Milton Keynes in Settings and wait for Ready for offline journeys. Do this on the device you will take with you.
  4. If you planned on a computer, open the route link on your phone before leaving. Check the start pin and destination there.
